# Ownership and contributor requirements

This document records a process requirement; it is not an assignment, employment agreement, contractor agreement, or legal opinion.

Before treating a contribution as exclusively controlled first-party VELORI material:

1. Identify the contributor and the specific work or deliverables.
2. Obtain and retain an executed written assignment or a licence broad enough for the intended use, from a person with authority to grant it.
3. Record any pre-existing materials, third-party components, open-source dependencies, assets, templates, or generated output excluded from that grant.
4. Verify that the contributor had permission to include those materials and preserve any notices and attribution conditions.
5. Record the evidence location and review date in the IP inventory without committing confidential contracts or personal information.
6. Escalate unclear ownership, work-for-hire status, moral rights, or third-party restrictions for legal review.

For Figma Make or other generated output, retain the project/export identifiers and the terms applicable on the relevant date. Separate generated original material from any embedded third-party asset or code. Tool use alone does not establish exclusive ownership or commercial redistribution rights.
