# Acceptable Use

> Legal review draft specification. Not an approved policy or a compliance assessment. No new legal commitments, statutory timelines, officer identities or governing-law clauses are published by this file.

> Implementation status: the current public documentation release is deployed as version 20. The expanded workflow requirements below are not all implemented. New relational tables are a review-only proposal; no migration has been applied. This document is not evidence of a deployed API, legal approval, live vacancy, delivered email or translated platform.

Respect people
Do not harass, threaten, exploit, defraud or impersonate people. Protect private information and respect another person’s boundaries.

Respect access
Do not enter accounts or data that are not yours, evade restrictions or disrupt the service.

Respect content
Only upload materials you have permission to share. Do not distribute malicious files or use the platform for unlawful activity.

Raise a concern
Use available reporting controls for a specific content item or message. Contact the team for a wider question.

## Acceptable Use Policy

Prohibit unauthorized access, attacks, credential theft, malicious automation, abusive scraping, spamming, manipulation of engagement systems and misuse of platform APIs.

Define approved automated access through the developer program.

## Review and release controls

Identify the responsible legal entity and relevant jurisdictions, verify actual product processing, obtain authorized professional review and record the exact approved content hash, language, reviewer and effective date. The schema proposal records these decisions but does not itself enforce a CMS publication gate. Connect and test that gate before enabling the workflow. Do not relabel an unreviewed draft as effective.

## Validation

Check public/private behavior against the implementation, policy contacts against Site settings, all request links against actual supported workflows, and accessibility in Full/Gentle/Off. Store previous approved revisions and publish meaningful change notices only when approved.
