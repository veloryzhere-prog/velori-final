# Community Guidelines

> Legal review draft specification. Not an approved policy or a compliance assessment. No new legal commitments, statutory timelines, officer identities or governing-law clauses are published by this file.

> Implementation status: the current public documentation release is deployed as version 20. The expanded workflow requirements below are not all implemented. New relational tables are a review-only proposal; no migration has been applied. This document is not evidence of a deployed API, legal approval, live vacancy, delivered email or translated platform.

Respect the person
Disagree with ideas without harassment, threats, hate or humiliation. Do not target someone with repeated unwanted contact.

Share what you have a right to share
Respect copyright and privacy. Do not post another person’s private contact details, sensitive information or conversations without permission.

Be honest about who you are
Do not impersonate another person, deceive people about an offer or manipulate account access.

Keep the platform safe
Do not distribute malicious files, evade account controls or exploit others. Use safety and reporting tools for concerns.

Help improve the space
A useful report includes context and the relevant content. Avoid reposting harmful or private material publicly to make a complaint.

## Community Guidelines

**Opening statement:**

"VELORI is intended to be a space where people can create, communicate and participate with respect for others.

Our Community Guidelines explain expectations for content and conduct. They apply to public interactions and, where appropriate and legally permissible, other uses of the service."

Write separate rules for:
- Harassment and bullying.
- Threats and violent content.
- Hate and discrimination.
- Sexual exploitation and child safety.
- Privacy violations.
- Impersonation.
- Scams and deceptive behavior.
- Spam and manipulation.
- Intellectual property.
- Harmful misinformation where covered by policy.
- Unauthorized automation.
- Reporting and enforcement.

Describe proportional enforcement, contextual review and appeals.

## Review and release controls

Identify the responsible legal entity and relevant jurisdictions, verify actual product processing, obtain authorized professional review and record the exact approved content hash, language, reviewer and effective date. The schema proposal records these decisions but does not itself enforce a CMS publication gate. Connect and test that gate before enabling the workflow. Do not relabel an unreviewed draft as effective.

## Validation

Check public/private behavior against the implementation, policy contacts against Site settings, all request links against actual supported workflows, and accessibility in Full/Gentle/Off. Store previous approved revisions and publish meaningful change notices only when approved.
