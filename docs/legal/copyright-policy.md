# Copyright and proprietary-use policy (draft)

**Status:** Internal policy draft; rights-holder and legal review required.

## Intended approach

VELORI is the product; VELORYZ is the project owner's intended parent-brand and rights-holder identity. For work verified as eligible first-party material, the preferred notice is:

> Copyright © 2026 VELORYZ. All rights reserved.

The notice year is not evidence that all existing work was authored in 2026. Preserve historically accurate dates where documented. Do not use the notice on third-party, imported/generated, mixed-origin, or uncertain work unless the rights evidence supports it.

## Use and distribution

The intended model for verified first-party code is proprietary distribution, subject to adoption of the draft [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) after legal review. The policy does not apply to third-party materials, and it cannot override their licences. Do not describe minification, hidden routes, or a copyright header as preventing inspection or copying.

## Review process

Maintain the machine-generated [inventory](./intellectual-property-inventory.md), resolve unknown origins, review dependency and asset terms, and record evidence before applying first-party headers or declaring a release cleared. Update the inventory after significant source, asset, dependency, or provenance changes.

## Reporting

No verified infringement-reporting contact or legal address is available in this repository. The rights holder must approve a contact and response process before this policy is published as a public legal policy.
